# New Continent: Vespera

A haiku about the continent, in Ithkuil (creative approximation):

Vespera rises,  
Golden horizons spread wide,  
Peaceful land sleeps.

Ithkuil-inspired transliteration (Latin script, simplified morphology):
Line 1: *Vespera sîl kóv* (Vespera rises/elevates)
Line 2: *Xanthea tîl sîl* (Continents/lands spread forth)
Line 3: *Pax vea tûr* (Peaceful land abides)

English original haiku:
Vespera rises,
